Say goodbye to the hassle of heavy, cumbersome picnic tables and hello to The Freedom Table! Made from recyclable and reusable cardboard, this lightweight picnic table is perfect for all your outdoor adventures. Take it with you to the park, the beach, or for a joyous National Day Celebration!

The Freedom Table is easy to pack up and take with you wherever you go. Plus, it's coated so you don't have to worry about spills.

Comes laminated with a splash-proof surface.
